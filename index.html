THE STARTING QUESTION

What happens to the person in the workflow when AI becomes a contributor to cognitive work?

After 12+ years working across enterprise technology, organisational change, product and AI-enabled workflows, I became increasingly interested in a question delivery metrics could not answer.

Not simply what can AI do? — but what changes in human learning, judgement and agency when part of the thinking moves elsewhere.

— Tasneem Fatima

BEHIND THE STUDY

The responses behind the analysis.

This exploratory study began offline. Participants recorded responses by hand before and after reading the standardised AI response. These anonymised sheets make the path from raw response to interpretation visible.

THEN THE INTERPRETATION HIT A WALL

But there
was a problem.

01

Priming?

The AI response itself suggested an independent-first approach.

02

Memory?

Participants may simply have remembered ideas they had just read.

03

Existing beliefs?

Some responses may reflect views participants already held.

04

Actual learning?

No objective recall, retention, transfer or cognitive-load measure was used.

This study cannot tell us which explanation is correct.

And that made the next question sharper.
